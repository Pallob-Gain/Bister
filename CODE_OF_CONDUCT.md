# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Bister to be a technically rigorous, welcoming open-source project where disagreement about architecture is normal and respectful.

Participants are expected to:

- critique ideas rather than people;
- explain technical disagreements with evidence when possible;
- welcome questions and early-stage contributors;
- avoid harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attacks;
- respect privacy and confidentiality;
- give credit for others' work and ideas.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, doxxing, sexual harassment, and repeated hostile behavior after being asked to stop.

## Scope

This applies in Bister repositories, issue trackers, discussions, code review, project chat, events, and other spaces where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate these expectations. Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Reports should be made privately to the repository owner or designated maintainers rather than posted publicly when privacy is important.

## Attribution

This policy is intentionally concise and follows common open-source community conduct principles. It may later be replaced by a formally adopted community standard as project governance matures.
